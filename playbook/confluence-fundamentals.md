# Confluence: fundamentals and news

Fundamentals set the **background bias** and the **danger windows**. They are never a trade trigger on their own. Relationships between markets weaken or flip at times; the agent treats them as context, not rules.

## Gold (XAUUSD)
| Driver | Usual relationship |
|---|---|
| US dollar (DXY) | Usually inverse: a stronger dollar tends to weigh on gold |
| US real yields (10-year TIPS) | Usually inverse: higher real yields raise the cost of holding non-yielding gold |
| Fed rate expectations | Expected cuts tend to help gold; hawkish surprises tend to hurt it |
| Risk-off / geopolitics | Safe-haven demand tends to help gold |
| Central-bank buying, ETF flows | Slow background drivers, rarely intraday |

## High-impact US releases (New York time)
| Event | Usual time |
|---|---|
| CPI, PCE, Nonfarm Payrolls, Retail Sales, weekly Jobless Claims | 08:30 |
| ISM surveys | 10:00 |
| FOMC decision / press conference | 14:00 / 14:30 |

Check the economic calendar every day. On TradingView, the calendar is in the side panel, and event markers appear on the chart's time axis.

## Oil (USOIL / WTI)
- OPEC+ decisions, Middle East and Russia supply news, the US dollar, and global growth.
- Inventory data: **API on Tuesday at 16:30** and **EIA on Wednesday at 10:30** (New York time).

## Bitcoin (BTCUSD)
- Trades 24/7. Weekend liquidity is thinner, and gaps and spikes are more common.
- Drivers: risk appetite (it often moves with tech stocks, though not always), US spot Bitcoin ETF flows, regulation news, and dollar liquidity.

## Agent rule
If a high-impact event is within the no-trade window set in `risk/rules.md`, or a chart event marker sits on the current candle, flag it before any signal.
